- **Chiseled Plaque**: Dynamic high-resolution brushed silver plate engraved with `JCD PERFUMES IMPERIAL EXTRAIT 35%`.

- **Interactive Atomizer Spray**: Click **"TEST ATOMIZER SPRAY"** to trigger a physical cone of 350+ glowing silver and sapphire mist particles accompanied by a luxury crystal chime.
- **Live Colorway Switcher**: Switch between *Silver & Sapphire Imperial*, *Midnight Noir Oud*, *L'Éclat de Platine*, and *Crown Amber*.
